Early-stage technical research

Building clearer digital records for energy.

WATTERRA explores how software can organize energy-project data, supporting evidence and verification records in a transparent, auditable information system.

01 / Data

Project registry

A structured information model for energy projects, participants and supporting documentation.

02 / Verification

Evidence first

A design approach based on traceable evidence, documented sources and explicit verification states.

03 / Records

Rights data research

Research into how records associated with energy projects may be represented, tracked and reconciled. A digital record alone does not create or prove a real-world right.

Current development status

Prototype and specifications in progress
This is an early-stage project. Public materials describe research and prototypes only; no production network deployment, operational registry or live energy integration is claimed.

Technical principles

Auditability · Evidence traceability · Clear verification states · Separation of software records from real-world energy delivery and legal rights · Independent review before operational use.
